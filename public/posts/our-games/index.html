Essom + Hamla⌗

For our first kickstarter we feature two games: Hamla and Essom. Both games were made on Gotland, and they share the award for Best Game Design at the Gotland Game Conference 2025. As the Designers put it: “We are friends that encouraged each other to present our games at the conference, so we see it as a very fitting ending to share the award”.

Curious about the games?



Essom⌗

Players: 2 | Age: 10+ | Time: 25’

Summary.

Essom is an abstract game design by Guido Salustri, a game designer from Argentina. Essom combines hidden information with tactical depth, where players need to search for and retrieve hidden tokens.

Hamla⌗

Players: 2-6 | Age: 8+ | Time: 15’

Summary.

Hamla is a card game design by Márton Bodrog, a game designer from Hungary. Coming from the world of Trutina, Hamla is a game of cunning, bluffing and a bit of luck, where you take the role of commanders preparing for battle.
