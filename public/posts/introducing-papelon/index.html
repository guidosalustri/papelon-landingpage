About Us⌗

We are a 3-person board game studio based in the historic island town of Visby, Gotland (the very place where we learned to make games). We love collaborating with designers and artists, and above all, we want more people to play and create games. And that’s why we think print-and-play is so great!

Take a 2 min tour around Gotland with us to learn more about our studio and see where we’re heading next!!

The Tour⌗

A Rising Tide Lifts all Boats

We want to support emerging, diverse talent joining the board game industry. Because it’s algebraic: more diverse game crafters lead to more diverse crafted games ;)

The Hidden Power of Format

Print-and-play makes games affordable, instantly accessible to everyone, and uniquely personal. You don’t just play the game, you spend time crafting it, too. And as we all know: "It is the time you spend on your game that makes your game so important." Go, go, PnP!

Nothing New Under the Sun

Board games are great for having fun, but they can also create a safe space to foster integration, emotional regulation and cognitive skills. This gives so much exciting room to explore and create!
